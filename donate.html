Help fund my master's degree

I am pursuing a Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology at Yorkville University while working full time in mental health services in Calgary. Balancing graduate school with the demands of full-time work is not easy. It requires long hours of reading, writing, research, and engaging in discussion groups, often after a full day of supporting people in mental health services.

As someone who is visually impaired, these demands come with additional challenges. Reading academic materials, completing assignments, and keeping up with graduate-level expectations require extra time, effort, and accessible tools. There are days when the workload feels overwhelming, but I remain committed to my education and the difference I hope to make.

My journey is also about advocacy. I care deeply about disability rights, accessibility, and improving mental health care. I want to use my education and lived experiences to help create a more inclusive and compassionate society.

Your donation would make a meaningful difference. It would help ease the financial pressure of putting a roof over my head while pursuing graduate studies and managing the costs that come with this journey. Every contribution, no matter the amount, would help lighten that burden.

Thank you for believing in my journey and supporting my goal of making a difference.
