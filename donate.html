Support my studies

Help fund my master's degree

I am pursuing a Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology at Yorkville University while working full time in mental health services in Calgary. It is a demanding path, and balancing both responsibilities is not easy.

Graduate school requires more than attending lectures and completing assignments. It means hours of reading, research, critical thinking, academic writing, and meeting deadlines. There are papers to write, theories to understand, research to evaluate, and new ideas to put into practice. I often move from a full day of supporting people in mental health services to evenings spent studying, reading, and working on assignments. Finding enough time and energy to do both is a constant challenge.

I also navigate these demands while living with a visual impairment. Reading academic materials, reviewing research articles, working through lengthy documents, and completing assignments can take considerably more time and effort. Tasks that may be straightforward for other students often require additional planning, assistive technology, magnification, and breaks. I have to work harder to access the same information and meet the same academic expectations.

Despite these challenges, I remain committed to my education and to the work I care deeply about. I advocate for people with disabilities, better mental health services, accessibility, and equal opportunities. I want to use my education and lived experience to help make mental health care more inclusive and accessible, particularly for people whose needs are often overlooked.

Your donation would help ease the financial pressure of graduate school and make it easier to manage tuition, textbooks, academic resources, and the additional tools needed to study with a visual impairment. It will help ease the financial demand of putting a roof over my head while in graduate school.

I am not asking for an easy path. I am asking for support in making a demanding one more manageable. Every contribution, regardless of size, would make a meaningful difference. Thank you for helping me continue this journey.
