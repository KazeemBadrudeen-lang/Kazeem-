I am working toward a Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology at Yorkville University, online, while working full time in mental health services in Calgary. I have spent fourteen years on the front line, supporting people with disabilities and mental health challenges. This degree is how I take that work further, into counselling.

Tuition is a real cost alongside full-time work. If my work, my writing, or my free resources have helped you, a contribution of any size helps keep me in school. Thank you for standing behind this next step.
