PlayHaven — Real Rooms

PlayHaven is a place to play board and card games with friends or on your own. Create a room, share its five-letter code, and invite someone to join from another device. No account needed. Just choose a display name and play.

How real rooms work: Bid and Trick and Five Suit Meld support real multiplayer games with two players on different devices. One player creates a room and shares its five-letter code. The host's browser checks the moves and keeps the game running for both players. If the host leaves the room, the game ends. All other games are played solo or against the computer in your own browser. This page is not an App Store listing.

Bid and Trick: Play five rounds of a trick-taking card game where each round brings a new challenge. Bid on how many tricks you think you can win, then play your cards carefully. Watch for the Star and Fool cards, and score points by matching your exact bid.

Five Suit Meld: Build sets and runs over five rounds, using three to seven cards depending on the round. The wild rank changes each round, and Jokers can help complete your melds. Keep your leftover cards low because the player with the lowest total score wins.

Four in a Row: Take turns dropping pieces into the board and try to connect four in a row. Plan your moves, block your opponent, and look for a chance to win. Play against the computer.

Blackjack: Try to get as close to 21 as possible without going over. Decide when to take another card or stand with what you have. Play against the computer and see how your hand compares.

Tic-Tac-Toe: Take turns placing Xs and Os on a three-by-three grid. Get three in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. Play against the computer and test your strategy.

Memory Match: Turn over cards and look for matching pairs. Remember where each picture is as you work through the board. Play at your own pace and see how quickly you can find every match.

Rules, in brief

Bid and Trick (5 rounds, 1 to 5 cards): Each round, bid how many tricks you will win, then play tricks. Follow the led suit if you can; Star and Fool cards may be played any time. The first Star played wins a trick; a Fool only wins if every card in the trick is a Fool. Trump beats the led suit. If the trump card drawn is a Star or Fool, it is redrawn until a suit card appears. An exact bid scores 20 + 10 per trick won; a missed bid loses 10 per trick of difference. Highest total after 5 rounds wins.

Five Suit Meld (5 rounds, 3 to 7 cards): The wild rank changes each round: 3 in round 1, 4 in round 2, and so on. Jokers are always wild. Draw, then lay melds if you wish, then discard to end your turn. A meld is 3 or more cards of the same rank (a set) or 3 or more cards of the same suit in sequence (a run), with wild cards filling gaps. To go out, your whole hand after discarding must form melds. Your opponent then gets one last turn. Unmelded cards count as deadwood: number cards at face value, Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13, wild cards 20, Jokers 50. Lowest deadwood total after 5 rounds wins.

Limits of this version: Real multiplayer is for 2 players only, in Bid and Trick and Five Suit Meld. Tic-Tac-Toe, Four in a Row, Blackjack and Memory Match are playable in this browser against the computer or solo — they are not synced to the other player. If the host disconnects, the room ends.
