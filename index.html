About

My Story

My commitment to health equity isn't just professional, it's personal. Growing up with albinism, I watched my single, working mother navigate a healthcare system that often felt like a barrier rather than a bridge. I witnessed the lost income and the burden of prolonged wait times that many marginalized families face today.

That lived experience set the direction of my career. For 14 years I have worked in health and human services, most of that time in community rehabilitation and mental health recreation. I run recreation services at Elements Calgary Mental Health, where I design and facilitate programs that bring people together, build skills, and open doors to community life.

I am currently completing a Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology at Yorkville University, building on my Master of Health Studies (Leadership) from Athabasca University and my Bachelor of Community Rehabilitation from the University of Calgary.

The purpose of this website is to document my professional development in a consistent manner. More importantly, to offer you, prospective employer, educator, colleague, an avenue to get a glimpse of who I am, my values, beliefs, philosophies, and educational background.

Philosophy and Practice

Dismantling Barriers

As a Health and Human Services professional, my primary objective is to advocate for systemic social change. I am dedicated to dismantling the oppressive structures that marginalize individuals with disabilities. My work is rooted in the belief that systems should empower, not merely manage, the people they serve.

Core Pillars of My Practice

Person-Directed Advocacy

I believe in shifting the power back to the individual. By placing marginalized voices at the forefront of decision-making, we move beyond inclusion and toward true citizenship and autonomy.

The Power of Unpaid Relationships

A life lived entirely among paid professionals is a life in isolation. I prioritize helping individuals cultivate authentic, unpaid community connections.

Self-Reflective Excellence

I am committed to continuous self-reflection to recognize and mitigate personal biases, ensuring my practice remains as objective and impactful as possible.

Ethical Integrity and Trust

Trust: the individual's desires remain central.
Transparency: accountability to prevent the re-emergence of oppressive structures.
Equity: actively redistributing power to amplify the voices of those I serve.
