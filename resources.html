About this directory

This directory was compiled in October 2026 from the public listings of the organisations themselves and Alberta service directories. Phone numbers and websites can change — if a number does not work, call 2-1-1 and ask for the service by name, or check the organisation's website for its current contact details.

Inclusion here is not an endorsement, and services set their own eligibility rules. In an emergency, always call 911.
